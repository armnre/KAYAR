# KAYAR

Imported unchanged from github.com/armnre/KAYAR (`kayar-zite/apps/kayar`). Persian-language members app: workout & nutrition coaching, music/podcast library (Morshed), campaigns, shop and a staff admin panel. Members sign in with the app's own username/password login managed by staff.

Deployment helpers from the repo that are not part of the Zite app (standalone server, relay worker, Docker/Liara files) are kept under `/repo-extras`.
